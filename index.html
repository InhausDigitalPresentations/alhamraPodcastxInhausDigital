Part one

The shoot. Every face. Every reaction. Cameras that disappear into the room.

I
The reference

The industry roundtable, moved into a private dining room.

The format the screen industry uses to film its biggest names in conversation: a table, a few chairs, and coverage so complete that no line and no glance is lost.

▶Reference · THR Drama Actors Roundtable

Format reference only. Footage © The Hollywood Reporter. Watch: youtube.com/watch?v=EC5R1Rt2sWw

01Front shot of every seat. No profiles, no backs of heads. Every guest is seen speaking.
02A reaction for every line. A dedicated camera hunts the listener. Reactions sell the moment.
03The table is the stage. A wide that holds the whole evening together, from the open to the last word.
04Nobody performs. If guests can see the crew, they talk to the crew. So they won't see us.
Invisible by design

Part of the room. Not in the way.

3 mComfort radius. No camera, light or person inside it. Long lenses (85–135 mm) get the fronts from beyond. Final positions confirmed at the recce.
T–4hSet before they arrive. Every camera and light placed, framed on stand-ins and dressed into the décor before the first guest walks in.
0Tally lights, beeps or fans. Bodies on low plinths and shelving, finished to match the room.
1Light source: the room. Our lights are designed as lamps and candles. The set looks finished, not rigged.
Camera plan

Six cameras. Fixed once, identical for twelve episodes.

AMaster wide. High, behind the host. The whole table.
BHost front. 135 mm over G3's shoulder.
CGuest fronts, G1 · G2. Cross-shot from the far wall.
DGuest fronts, G4 · G5 · G3. Cross-shot, mirrored.
EReaction hunter. Long lens, one quiet operator at the room's edge.
FRoaming detail. Hands, glasses, the menu. Operated, moving quietly along the room's edge.

A–D locked off and framed before guests arrive. E and F operated discreetly; F is the roaming camera. Positions confirmed at the recce. 6K capture, 4K delivery: room to reframe 1:1 or a group of five without touching a camera.

Four shot types. Every minute covered.

The kit. Per episode.

Part two

The chapter system. One evening becomes a month of content.

II
The idea

Most podcasts are cut after they're recorded. Ours is designed to be cut before.

Each conversation is built in four chapters, one per theme in your brief. Every chapter follows the same anatomy, so every chapter delivers the same set of assets.

1 · QuestionThe hook→ opens the short
2 · StoryThe proof→ the article section
3 · TensionThe debate→ the reaction cut
4 · TakeawayThe line→ the quote card
I The marketII The growth storyIII The opportunityIV The vision+ The last word: one closing question, every guest, every episode
